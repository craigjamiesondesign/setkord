Questions, answered

Visit the help centre
How much does it cost?
Free to download and use, and you'll always be able to log workouts. Setkord Pro (monthly or annual) adds unlimited routines, your full training history and advanced insights.
What does it run on?
iPhone, on the App Store now (iOS 17 or later).
Does it work without signal?
Yes. Every set saves on your phone with no connection and syncs when you're back online.
Can I bring my old workouts?
Yes. Import your history from Strong or Hevy with a CSV, and export your data to CSV any time.
Is my data private?
Yes. We only collect anonymous analytics, and you can export or delete your data anytime. See our privacy policy for details.
Is there an Android version?
Not yet. If you'd use it, tell us through the contact page.
