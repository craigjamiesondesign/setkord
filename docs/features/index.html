Logging

Everything about capturing a set is built to take seconds, not menus.

  • Unlimited workouts

    Log as much as you train. There is no cap on sessions, on Free or Pro.

  • Three set types

    Weight x reps, bodyweight (reps only), or timed (mm:ss) for planks, carries and cardio.

  • Fast set entry

    Log weight x reps in a tap or two, with your last session's numbers pre-filled.

  • Per-set notes

    Drop a note on any set - "felt easy", "left elbow twinge" - and see it inline later.

  • RPE per set

    An optional 1-5 effort rating on each set, switched on in preferences.

  • Warm-up sets

    Flag warm-ups so they never inflate your working volume or steal a PR.

  • Warm-up calculator

    Builds a percentage ramp to your working weight, rounded to loadable plates or dumbbells.

  • Plate calculator

    Shows exactly what to load per side of the bar for any target weight, in kg or lb.

  • Supersets & giant sets

    Group exercises with clear on-screen separation so alternating stays as fast as a straight set.

  • Reorder exercises

    Drag exercises into any order, mid-workout.

  • Per-exercise rest override

    Give one exercise its own rest length without touching your default.

  • Automatic rest timer

    Starts itself when you log a set, keeps running when your phone locks, and notifies you when it's done.

  • Live session timer

    A running clock for the whole workout - on if you want it, off if you don't.

  • Back-date a workout

    Log a session on a past day for when you forgot to track it in the moment.

  • Minimize & resume

    Step out of a workout mid-session and pick it back up from the Train screen.

  • Dumbbell handling

    Enter the combined weight you actually lifted; volume is always tracked as true load.

  • Distance tracking

    Optionally log distance on loaded carries and cardio - in metres/km or feet/miles - as its own record, never folded into your lifting volume. Off by default.

  • Save as a routine

    Turn any finished workout into a reusable routine in one tap.

  • Finish summary

    A clean end-of-session recap - total volume (or cardio time), sets, time, and any PRs - and share it straight away as a card, full image or PDF.

  • Works offline

    Every set logs instantly with no signal and syncs the moment you're back online.

Exercises

A big, clean catalog - and room for everything you actually train.

  • 441-exercise catalog

    Browse by muscle group or search; the library is updated over the air, no app update needed.

  • Unlimited custom exercises

    Type any exercise name and it's added - tracked like a first-class movement.

  • Manage your exercises

    Rename a lift in place, delete a custom one, or flag it as a two-dumbbell exercise - all from one place.

  • Curate your catalog

    Hide any built-in you never use, or browse only your own exercises for a clean, personal list. An explicit search still finds everything.

  • Merge exercises

    Fold one exercise's history into another to clean up duplicates.

  • Group variations

    Roll variations ("rdl" into "Romanian Deadlift") under one lift for stats, with a rolled-up-or-individual toggle.

  • Per-exercise detail

    Per-lift charts for estimated 1RM, heaviest weight and volume over time, with your full set history.

Routines & programs

Build your training once and start it in a tap - even with no signal.

  • Build routines

    Pick exercises, set target sets, reps and supersets, and reuse them session after session. Free covers up to 7 routines; Pro unlocks unlimited routines.

  • Routine folders

    Organise routines into groups so your training stays tidy.

  • Routine templates

    Start from ready-made routine templates instead of a blank slate.

  • Start empty or from a routine

    Launch a workout blank, or from a saved routine pre-filled and ready to log - fully offline.

  • Programs to follow

    A curated library of 36 proven multi-day programs - from Starting Strength and 5/3/1 to PPL, glute-focused and fat-loss plans - updated over the air, that you can start or save to your own.

  • Filter programs by goal

    Browse the built-in library by goal - build muscle, strength, fat loss or general - with the level and day count on every card.

  • Programs

    Group your routines into a program - push, pull, legs - so a whole block lives in one place.

Progress & stats

Every set you log feeds your numbers automatically. See the trend, not just today.

  • Training totals

    Volume, workouts, sets and your streak at a glance, with period-over-period trend arrows.

  • Automatic personal records

    PRs are detected as you log - against your all-time bests even on Free - and celebrated the instant you finish.

  • Estimated 1RM

    An estimated one-rep max for every lift, so you can watch your strength climb.

  • Muscle balance

    Working sets per body part, with gentle flags for the areas you've been skipping.

  • Top exercises

    Your most-trained lifts, ranked, so you can see where your work is actually going.

  • Weekly volume

    A snapshot of total weight moved per week.

  • Cardio conditioning

    Log cardio in minutes and see your weekly conditioning time and an 8-week trend - kept in its own lane, never mixed into your lifting volume.

  • Volume trendPro

  • Training consistencyPro

  • Rest ratioPro

  • Period comparisonPro

  • Plateau & fatigue signalsPro

History

Your full training record, exactly the way you want it.

  • Workout history

    Every session with full per-workout detail. Free shows the last 12 months; Pro unlocks all-time (list, stats and PRs).

  • List & calendar views

    Scroll your sessions as a list, or see your training as a month grid.

  • Search

    Find any past session by workout name or exercise.

  • Date-range filter

    Narrow history to this year or a custom from/to range.

  • Edit anything

    Open any past workout to rename it in place, then view, edit or delete its exercises, sets and notes.

Body measurements

Track the numbers beyond the bar.

  • Log your metrics

    Weight, body-fat %, and tape measurements (neck, chest, waist, hips, arms, thighs, calves), in your units.

  • Trend charts

    Each metric charted over time, with a range filter to zoom in or out.

  • Full control

    Add, edit or delete any measurement entry whenever you like.

Sharing

Show your work - and bring your training partners with you.

  • Share a workout image

    Turn a session into a branded card - your stats over your own photo, or a bold stat card - with a live preview before you post it anywhere.

  • Share a workout PDFPro

    Export the complete session - every exercise and set - as a branded PDF document.

  • Share a routinePro

    Send a routine or a whole group to another lifter via a link; they tap to import it. Creating a share is Pro; importing is open to everyone.

Import & export

Your data works wherever you do, and it's always yours.

  • Import from Strong & Hevy

    Bring your history across via CSV, so you start with your real numbers instead of a blank slate.

  • Export your data

    Download your entire training history to CSV any time. You own your data - Setkord just keeps it for you.

  • Reset your data

    Wipe your workouts, routines and PRs while keeping your account, if you ever want a clean start.

Sync & offline

Local-first: instant on your phone, in sync everywhere.

  • Local-first & offline

    The app works fully offline and never blocks on a connection - no spinners, no lost sets.

  • Automatic sync

    Your training syncs in the background across your devices and to the web the moment you're online.

Widgets, Siri & reminders

Setkord reaches out from the home screen, lock screen and Siri.

  • Home & lock-screen widgets

    Your week streak and this week's workout count, glanceable without opening the app.

  • Live Activity

    Your active workout and rest countdown on the lock screen and Dynamic Island.

  • Start with Siri

    "Hey Siri, start my Setkord workout" - or a Shortcut / Control Center button - starts or resumes a session, offline.

  • Training reminders

    A friendly nudge on the days and time you choose. On your phone only, opt-in, off by default.

Apple Health

Your lifting counts toward your day.

  • Save workouts to Apple Health

    Finished sessions write to Apple Health as strength training (one-way, opt-in), with an Open in Health shortcut.

Account & preferences

Set it up the way you train.

  • Email or Google sign-in

    Create an account in seconds - no card required to start.

  • Units

    Train in kilograms or pounds; everything follows your choice.

  • Default rest length

    Set the rest time that fits your training, from 30 seconds up.

  • Your plan

    Setkord is free to download and use. Pro is an optional monthly or annual subscription that you manage and restore from inside the app.

  • Email updates

    Opt in to product news and tips - unticked by default, unsubscribe any time.

  • Account controls

    Edit your name, replay the app tour, log out, or delete your account and its data outright.

Accessibility

Built to be readable and usable from day one, not bolted on later.

  • Dynamic Type

    The whole app scales with your iOS text-size setting, and the hierarchy holds at every size.

  • High-contrast mode

    A higher-contrast palette for surfaces, borders and text when you need it.

  • Text-size presets

    In-app Small to Huge presets on top of the system setting.

  • VoiceOver

    Labelled controls and actions throughout, including charts and destructive actions.
