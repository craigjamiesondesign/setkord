Hevy Alternatives: The Best Options for Lifters in 2026

2 September 2026 · Mark Stent

  • comparison
  • apps

If you are looking for a Hevy alternative, the good news is there are several genuinely good lifting trackers, and the right one depends on how you train. Strong is the proven, focused classic; Boostcamp is built around following structured programs; and Setkord is the local-first, strength-first option that works fully offline and imports your Hevy history for free. None of them is simply "better than Hevy" - they are better for different lifters, and this roundup tries to say which is which honestly.

I build Setkord, so treat this as a review from someone with a stake in it. I have kept everything about the other apps general and fair, because a roundup you cannot trust is not worth writing.

Why look past Hevy at all?

Hevy is a good app with a big, active community - plenty of lifters are perfectly happy on it. People usually go looking for an alternative for concrete reasons:

  • They want something that works reliably offline, with no spinner when signal drops.
  • They do not want a social feed and would rather a clean, single-purpose log.
  • They want deeper stats or a particular way of following programs.
  • They want their data to stay portable - easy to import in and export out.

If none of those apply, there is nothing wrong with staying on Hevy. If any of them do, here are the alternatives worth a look.

The best Hevy alternatives

Strong

Strong is one of the original serious lifting apps and it has aged well. It is focused, reliable and familiar, with a clean logging flow that a lot of experienced lifters have trusted for years. It does the core job without trying to be a lifestyle app or a social network.

Best for: lifters who want a proven, no-nonsense log and do not need a feed.

Boostcamp

Boostcamp's strength is structured programs. It offers a large library of well-known training programs you can follow step by step, which is genuinely useful if you would rather run a proven plan than build your own from scratch.

Best for: lifters who want to follow an established program with the plan laid out for them.

Setkord

Setkord is the one I build, so here is the honest pitch and the honest limits. It is local-first, which is its main point of difference: every set logs instantly with no signal, no spinner and nothing lost, then syncs when you are back online. It is strength-first and uncluttered - built for lifters, not for tracking steps or sleep, and with no social feed by design. You get fast logging with your last numbers pre-filled, automatic PRs and an estimated 1RM, routines and a curated program library, a plate and warm-up calculator, body measurements, deeper Pro Insights, a web app, and full accessibility (Dynamic Type and VoiceOver) from day one. Crucially for someone leaving Hevy: it imports your Hevy history via CSV for free, and lets you export everything back out any time. The core is free forever.

Where it does not fit: if you specifically want a social feed and a large community, Setkord is not that. It is also younger, so its community is smaller.

Best for: offline, strength-first lifters who want a clean log, deeper stats, and to own their data - with a painless move over from Hevy.
