Setkord vs Hevy: A Fair Head-to-Head for Lifters

2 September 2026 · Mark Stent

  • comparison
  • apps

Setkord and Hevy are both strong lifting trackers that aim at different lifters. Hevy is mature, polished, runs on almost everything and has a big social community, which is a genuine draw if a feed keeps you motivated. Setkord is strength-first and iPhone-first: no feed, no clutter, accessibility built in, and it imports your Hevy history for free - so if you want a fast, private, single-purpose log, that is where it fits.

I build Setkord, so read this as an honest comparison from someone on one side of it. Every claim about Hevy below comes from Hevy's own website or App Store listing, linked in the sources at the end, because getting a competitor's details wrong is the fastest way to lose your trust. Where Hevy's pages do not say something, I say that rather than guess.

The short version

Hevy is the better choice if you want a social feed, or you log on Android, Wear OS or an Apple Watch, because it runs on all of them and has a large, active community. Setkord is the better fit if you lift on an iPhone and want a clean, strength-first log with no feed, accessibility built in, more routines on the free plan and a free import of your Hevy or Strong history.

  • Pick Hevy if you want to follow friends and share workouts, use Android or a smartwatch, or want the lowest-priced Pro.
  • Pick Setkord if you are on iPhone and want a focused log with no social layer, built-in accessibility, seven free routines and unlimited custom exercises.

Both log sets, track lifts, show history, estimate your maxes and work offline. The differences are about focus, devices and how much the free plan covers.

Where Hevy is genuinely good

Hevy has earned its following. It is a well-built, polished app with a clean interface and a large, active community of lifters sharing routines and progress. If part of what keeps you training is the social side - following friends, seeing what others lifted, posting a PR - Hevy leans into that harder than Setkord does, because Setkord deliberately has no feed.

It also goes where Setkord does not yet: Android, Wear OS, and an Apple Watch app that logs a whole workout without your phone. Its free plan keeps your full workout history, and its Pro plan is priced low, with a lifetime option.

If a social feed or a smartwatch is a must-have for you, Hevy is the more natural home. I am not going to pretend otherwise.

Where Setkord is different

Strength-first, no clutter

Setkord is built for lifters and nothing else. There is no feed, no followers and no leaderboards, so the whole app stays focused on logging sets and watching your strength climb. That is a preference, not a verdict - some people want the community, some want the clean single-purpose tool. Setkord is firmly the latter.

Fully offline, local-first

Both apps keep working when the signal drops. Setkord is built local-first from the ground up: every set saves to your phone first, instantly, with no spinner, and the network is only ever used to sync in the background. Basements, rural gyms, aeroplane mode - the app never waits on a connection.

More on the free plan where it counts for lifters

Setkord's free plan gives you up to seven routines and unlimited custom exercises, so you can set up a real program and name every lift the way you do without paying. Hevy's free plan keeps your full workout history, which is generous; Setkord's free plan keeps the last 12 months, with all-time history in Pro.

Free import from Hevy and Strong

If you are already on Hevy, you are not starting over. Setkord imports your Hevy history via CSV for free, so you begin with your real numbers instead of a blank slate. It imports Strong history too, and you can export the whole lot back out to CSV any time.

Built-in accessibility

Setkord supports full Dynamic Type, a high-contrast mode and VoiceOver from day one, including on charts and destructive actions. Accessibility is often an afterthought in fitness apps; here it was in from the start.

Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and Siri

A live rest countdown sits on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, a widget keeps your streak and weekly volume on the Home Screen, and you can start training by saying "Hey Siri, start my Setkord workout."

Pricing: both start free

Both apps are free to download with unlimited workouts. Setkord's free plan covers up to seven routines and unlimited custom exercises; Hevy's covers four routines and seven custom exercises. Both sell an optional subscription in the app, and Hevy also offers a lifetime purchase. I will not quote prices here since they change and vary by region - check each App Store listing for the current numbers.

Which should you choose?

Be honest about what motivates you. If a community and a feed are what keep you coming back, or you train with an Android phone or a smartwatch, Hevy is a great fit and I would not talk you out of it. If you lift on an iPhone and want a clean strength-only log, built-in accessibility and more room on the free plan, Setkord is built for exactly that.

You do not have to guess blind, either: import your Hevy history into Setkord for free and run both for a couple of weeks.

FAQ

Is Setkord better than Hevy? Setkord is the better fit for iPhone lifters who want a focused, strength-first log with no social feed, built-in accessibility and more routines on the free plan. Hevy is the better pick if you want a social feed, or you log on Android, Wear OS or an Apple Watch.

What is the difference between Setkord and Hevy? Hevy is a multi-platform tracker with a big social community, running on iPhone, Android, Apple Watch, Wear OS and the web. Setkord is an iPhone-first, strength-first log with no feed, accessibility built in and a local-first design.

Can I import my Hevy data into Setkord? Yes. Setkord imports your Hevy history via CSV for free, so you start with your real numbers instead of a blank slate. It imports Strong history too, and you can export everything back to CSV any time.

Does Setkord have a social feed like Hevy? No, and that is deliberate. Setkord is strength-first with no feed, to keep the app fast and uncluttered. If a social community is important to you, Hevy is the stronger choice there.

Do Setkord and Hevy work offline? Yes, both do. Setkord is local-first, so every set saves to your phone instantly and syncs later; Hevy says it works offline and syncs once you have a connection.

Which free plan gives you more? It depends what you need. Setkord's free plan gives you more routines (seven versus four) and unlimited custom exercises (versus seven). Hevy's free plan keeps your full workout history, while Setkord's keeps the last 12 months.

Is Setkord free? The core is free forever - unlimited workouts, automatic PRs, estimated 1RM, routines and stats. Pro removes limits and adds deeper Insights, PDF export and routine sharing. Check current pricing in the App Store.
