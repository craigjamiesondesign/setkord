Setkord vs Strong: A Fair Head-to-Head for Lifters

2 September 2026 · Mark Stent

  • comparison
  • apps

Setkord and Strong are both focused lifting trackers, and Strong is one of the originals - proven, reliable and familiar to a lot of experienced lifters. Setkord is the newer, local-first option: it works fully offline with no spinners, it is strength-first with no clutter, it adds deeper stats and Pro Insights, and it imports your Strong history for free. If you want a modern offline log that you also own the data in, that is where Setkord fits; if you already trust Strong's simple, no-frills flow, it is a fair reason to stay.

I build Setkord, so read this as an honest comparison from one side of it. Every claim about Strong below comes from Strong's own website, help centre or App Store listing, linked in the sources at the end, because getting a competitor's details wrong is the fastest way to lose your trust. Where Strong's pages do not say something, I say that rather than guess.

The short version

Strong is the stronger choice if you log on Android or straight from an Apple Watch, because it runs on iPhone, Android and Apple Watch and syncs through a Strong account. Setkord is the better fit if you lift on an iPhone and train where signal drops, especially if you want fully offline logging, a free import of your Strong history and accessibility built in.

  • Pick Strong if you use Android, switch between Android and iPhone, or want to log from your Apple Watch without your phone.
  • Pick Setkord if you are on iPhone and want fully offline local-first logging, deeper progress stats and Insights, free import from Strong, built-in accessibility and a free forever core.

Both do the core job well: log sets, track lifts, see history, estimate maxes. The differences are in where you can log, offline behaviour, depth of stats and how portable your data is.

Where Strong is genuinely good

Strong has earned its reputation as one of the original serious lifting apps. It is focused, reliable and familiar - it logs sets cleanly and tracks your lifts without trying to be a lifestyle app or a social network. For a lot of experienced lifters it is the app they have used for years and never had a reason to leave, and there is real value in a tool that just works and gets out of the way.

It also goes where Setkord does not yet: Android, and a full Apple Watch app that logs with or without your phone. If either of those matters to you, Strong is the honest pick today.

If you already have years of history in Strong and are happy with it, "it works and I trust it" is a perfectly good reason to stay.

Where Setkord is different

Fully offline, local-first

Setkord is local-first: every set logs instantly on your phone with no signal, no spinner and nothing lost, then syncs in the background once you are back online. The app never blocks on a connection, which matters in basements and rural gyms where signal simply is not there.

Deeper progress and Insights

Beyond the basics, Setkord gives you automatic PRs, an estimated 1RM for every lift, muscle-balance stats, top exercises and weekly volume. Pro adds Insights: volume and strength-progression trends, training consistency, muscle-volume trends, a PR timeline, period comparisons, and plateau and fatigue signals that flag when a lift has stalled. If you like to see the trend and not just today's session, there is a lot here.

Free import from Strong

You do not lose your years of history. Export your workouts from Strong as a CSV and Setkord imports them for free, so you start with your real numbers. And you can export everything back out to CSV any time - your data stays yours.

Built-in accessibility

Full Dynamic Type, a high-contrast mode and VoiceOver are in from day one, including on charts and destructive actions.

Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and Siri

A live rest countdown sits on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, a widget keeps your streak and weekly volume on the Home Screen, and you can start training by saying "Hey Siri, start my Setkord workout."

A real web app

Signed-in lifters can manage their account at setkord.com, and Pro adds browsing and authoring your training in a browser that syncs straight to your phone.

Pricing: both start free

Both apps are free to download with unlimited workouts. Setkord's free tier covers up to seven routines; Strong's covers 3 templates. Both sell an optional monthly or annual subscription in the app. I will not quote prices here since they change and vary by region - check each App Store listing for the current numbers.

Which should you choose?

If you are on Android, or you want to log from an Apple Watch, choose Strong - Setkord does not offer those today. If Strong already does everything you need and you trust it, there is nothing wrong with staying. If you lift on an iPhone and want an app that keeps working with no signal, gives you deeper trend Insights, and lets you both import your old data and export it freely, Setkord is built for that.

The low-risk move: import your Strong history into Setkord for free and run them side by side for a couple of weeks. You are comparing on your own numbers, not a demo.

Does Setkord work offline? Yes. Setkord is local-first, so every set logs instantly in a basement gym with no signal and syncs the moment you are back online. The app never waits on a connection.
